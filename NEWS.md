# News sources — Wednesday, 18 November 2026

The image sums up the world news of 18 November 2026 in six stories,
plus one short footnote. Every story below was reported that day by
at least two separate outlets; the wording on the image is a summary
written for this entry, not a quotation from any one source.

Note: for stories that were still developing when the image was made
— above all the Gulf escalation and the Kerch Bridge attack — only
what was already known and reported on 18 November is stated here;
later updates are deliberately not claimed as that day's news.

## 1. The Gulf heats up between the U.S. and Iran

- American jets hit a Revolutionary Guards missile site on Qeshm
  Island, at the mouth of the Gulf (Reuters).
- Tehran said its forces struck Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait,
  which hosts U.S. troops, while Houthi drones were aimed at Saudi
  pipeline sites; Bahrain reported intercepting several attacks
  before dawn (Just Security Early Edition; Reuters).

## 2. Xi and Putin talk in Shenzhen before the Trump trilateral

- China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen
  during the APEC summit, a day before planned three-way talks with
  U.S. President Donald Trump (AFP / AP).
- Xi spoke out against trade protectionism; Putin criticised Western
  sanctions (Just Security Early Edition).

## 3. The Kerch Bridge is partly brought down

- Ukrainian drones and missiles partly destroyed the bridge that
  links Russia with annexed Crimea; one of its two arches fell into
  the strait below (Horizon world brief; Daily News, 18 Nov 2026).

## 4. Peltola takes Alaska — Democrats reach 56–44 in the Senate

- Democrat Mary Peltola beat Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in
  Alaska, giving the Democrats a projected 56–44 Senate majority —
  their ninth Senate flip of the midterms (AP / Alaska Public Media).

## 5. Ebola in Congo passes 11,000 cases

- The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has more
  than doubled in three months, passing 11,000 cases across 67 health
  zones in eight provinces — already the second-largest Ebola outbreak
  ever recorded. Children under five are the worst affected
  (Zark News daily briefing; Horizon).

## 6. The U.S. loses its measles elimination status

- The United States lost the measles elimination status it had held
  since 2000, after the Pan American Health Organization's
  verification commission found the virus had spread without a break
  for more than a year: 5,412 confirmed cases in 2026 as of 12 Nov,
  the highest yearly total since 1991, across 45 states, with
  10 deaths (Zark News; Horizon).

## Footnote. Voyager 1 crosses one light-day

- Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth on 18 Nov 2026 at
  5:16:07 a.m. Eastern — roughly 16 billion miles out, further than
  any human-made object has ever travelled (NASA Jet Propulsion
  Laboratory).
